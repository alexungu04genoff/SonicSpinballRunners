# Historical Rooftop experiment archive

Archived on 2026-11-09 at Alex's request to preserve completed work across chats. This branch retains the original experiment based on a77560a. Current approved production materials live on main; this archive does not replace them.

The archive contains the experiment source, structural maps, launchers, study notes, and 30 selected comparison images and validation reports. Raw animation frames, videos, logs and intermediate captures remain local and are excluded by captures/.gitignore. No original files were deleted.

Validation for this archival commit: compared the worktree with current main, checked the selected text files for common credential formats, and confirmed that source changes are limited to this experiment. Existing visual validation reports are preserved as historical evidence; the old experiment was not rerun or certified against the current production game.
