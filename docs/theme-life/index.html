Theme Life / Environmental Motion

Seven themes, three review states each. Production still launches C everywhere. A/B are implemented candidates, awaiting artistic approval. Lava Reef is excluded.

These are actual disk-loaded Prototype captures with frozen gameplay and deterministic environmental time. Main clips use the normal gameplay camera angle and zoom, moved to right focus (570, 500). Camera movement changes no Ball, Ring, Goal or mechanism positions. Spawn stills provide the primary overlap/readability check.

Each clip samples six seconds at 15 fps. They are not seamless loops. JPEG95 source frames were encoded to H.264; this portable package compresses stills for review. Diagnostic stills and posters are at 7.25 s, beyond the clip sample. Wide uses a separate zoom-0.6 review camera. Retro off is diagnostic; production Retro is authoritative.
